From the Founder’s Desk

“Dance is more than steps. It is a language, a discipline and a way of carrying culture forward.”

Upasana began with a simple wish: to give every student a place to learn, grow and shine. Every class is a chance to build confidence, make friends and discover something new about yourself.

Our performances remind us that art can also give back to the community. Thank you to our students, families and supporters. I look forward to dancing with you.

Leeza Mathew
Founder & Instructor
